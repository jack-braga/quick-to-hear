This is a workbook for preparing a Bible study, not a generator. It structures the work, prompts you, and checks what you write, but the study is yours: it never writes your content for you.

<!-- expandable -->
Paper can hold your notes, but it cannot do the two things this tool is built for. First, it enforces disciplines you would otherwise skip when you are tired: it will not let a question through until you have written the answer you expect. Second, it carries your earlier work forward, so what you noticed in one phase comes back as raw material in the next rather than being lost. Everything it does serves your thinking. It will not think for you, and it will not hand you questions, a theme, or an application you did not work out yourself. That struggle is not a flaw in the tool; it is the training.
