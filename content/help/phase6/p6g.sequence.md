<!-- inline -->
Work in clusters: two or three observations, then the meaning question they can now answer. Keep application to the end.

<!-- expandable -->
The rule is observation before meaning, but for a given piece of text, not across the whole study. So you do not front-load every observation question and save all the meaning ones for later; you build a small cluster, two or three observations, then the meaning question those observations have just made answerable, and move on. Application stays at the end, because the study has one aim and everything has been leading there. The tool warns you if a meaning question sits ahead of the observations anchored to the same verses.
