<!-- inline -->
When you send the group to another passage, plan the question that brings them back. This is the step everyone forgets, and it is why studies so often finish in the wrong book.

<!-- expandable -->
A support passage pulls attention away from your text, and without a way back the discussion tends to settle wherever it landed. So for every passage you send them to, write the follow-up that returns them to the main text: what does this help us see back in our passage? The group should end the study in the book you set out to study, not in the one you visited along the way.
