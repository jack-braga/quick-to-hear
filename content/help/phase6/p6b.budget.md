<!-- inline -->
Your duration sets a rough question budget. Err low: finishing early having landed the aim beats rushing the last few questions so the application never happens.

<!-- expandable -->
As a guide, 45 minutes is about 6 to 8 questions, and an hour about 8 to 12. A rough spread across the types: context 0 to 1, observation 3 to 5, meaning 2 to 3, application 2 to 3, plus a prayer point. Each question carries a weight, light for about a minute, medium about three, heavy about six, and each support passage costs 3 to 5 minutes; a running total sits against your session length as you build. Treat all of this as a starting point rather than a rule, and when in doubt keep it low. A study that finishes early having reached its aim has done its work; one that rushes the last four questions usually means no application happened at all.
