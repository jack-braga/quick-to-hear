<!-- inline -->
COMA is four angles for your own preparation, not a plan for the meeting. Your group never sees these headings; they are scaffolding for your thinking.

<!-- expandable -->
COMA stands for Context, Observation, Meaning, and Application: four passes over the passage that keep you from jumping straight from reading to a lesson. Work them in your notes, not in the room. They shape how you prepare; they are not the shape of the study you build in Phase 6. *— after David Helm, One-to-One Bible Reading*
