Read the passage three or four times, at least once aloud, before you do anything else. No commentaries yet.

<!-- expandable -->
The aim here is to observe before you interpret, and that takes more than one reading. Read it slowly. Read it aloud at least once, because your ear catches what your eye slides over: repetitions, rhythm, where the weight falls. Keep the commentaries and study notes shut for now; they will still be there later, and reaching for them too soon lets someone else do your looking for you. First meet the text itself.
