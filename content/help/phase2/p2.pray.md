<!-- inline -->
Pray before you begin. We are trying to hear what God has said, and we need his help to hear it rightly.

<!-- expandable -->
This is not a formality. The same Spirit who moved the writers is the one who opens our eyes to what they wrote, and he works through the word rather than around it. So ask him: to quiet what you brought to the passage, to show you what is really there, and to change you by it, not only to inform you. Pray now, before the work, and you will find yourself praying again as you go.
