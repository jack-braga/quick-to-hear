<!-- inline -->
Mark anything you do not understand, whether a word, a phrase, or a whole verse. What confused you will confuse your group.

<!-- expandable -->
This marking is not idle; it feeds forward. In Phase 6 these confusions come back as candidate background boxes, short notes you hand the group, rather than questions you ask them. The rule of thumb is: what confused you, tell them; do not make them guess it. So mark honestly now. A difficulty you quietly step over at this stage tends to become a stumble in the room later, when someone else trips on the very thing you left unexamined.
