<!-- inline -->
Divide the passage into sections, following the author's own breaks rather than the chapter and verse numbers. Give each section a short heading in your own words.

<!-- expandable -->
The breaks you want are the author's, not the printer's. Look for the shifts that mark them: a change of time, place, or speaker, a new stage in the argument, a connective like "therefore" or "but now" that turns a corner. Where those fall is where your sections should fall. Keep each heading short and in your own words, just enough to hold the shape of the passage in view while you work.
