<!-- inline -->
Who is in the group. This shapes your application, and whether the tool will ask you for a question that makes the gospel plain.

<!-- expandable -->
Three settings: believers, a mixed group, or one-to-one with someone who is not yet a Christian. It is not a label on people; it changes what the study needs. For a mixed group or a not-yet-Christian, Phase 7 will require at least one question that makes the gospel plain, because you cannot assume everyone already understands it. It also steers application: the same passage is applied differently to the settled believer and to the person still weighing it up.
