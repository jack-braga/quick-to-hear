<!-- inline -->
Check the parsed text before moving on. Parsers get things wrong, and thirty seconds here saves the whole study.

<!-- expandable -->
Pasting rarely comes through perfectly. Look over the verse numbers and boundaries, the poetry line breaks (these collapse into prose most easily, and they carry the parallelism you will study), the section headings (which should be marked as editorial, not treated as the text), and the translation name. Fix anything wrong now. A silent parsing error is far worse than half a minute of checking, because your sections, notes, and questions all anchor to this text.
