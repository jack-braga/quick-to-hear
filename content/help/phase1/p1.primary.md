<!-- inline -->
Your primary translation is the one the group will hold. Everything anchors to it: verse references, questions, and the handout.

<!-- expandable -->
Choose the translation your group will actually read from. The tool ties verse numbers, question anchors, and the printed handout to this one text, so it is worth setting deliberately. You can add other translations for comparison at any time, but they stay secondary.
