Genre is the kind of writing this is. We have guessed it from the reference; check it, because it sets which COMA prompts you get and shapes how you read.

<!-- expandable -->
The six kinds are Gospels and Acts, Old Testament narrative, Epistles, Hebrew wisdom literature and poetry, Prophetic literature, and Apocalyptic literature. Genre matters because a letter is not read like a psalm, and a story is not read like a law. Your choice here decides which set of COMA prompts appears in Phase 4 and the reading tips you are shown, so if our guess is wrong, change it. When a passage mixes kinds, choose the one that governs the whole.
