<!-- inline -->
Faithfulness is not the same as certainty. Several themes could fairly be defended from this passage, and landing among them is faithful, even if another careful reader would choose slightly differently.

<!-- expandable -->
Most people stall here because they are waiting to feel certain, and certainty rarely comes. The goal was never the one perfect sentence, only a faithful one. Picture a range: some themes clearly fit the passage, some clearly do not, and a spread of defensible ones sits in between. Landing at a different point from another thoughtful reader is not unfaithfulness. Saying something the passage will not bear is. So write the sentence and move on. You can sharpen it later, but you cannot sharpen a blank page. This is where most people give up, and usually the fear is being slightly wrong, not the real risk of being badly wrong.
