<!-- inline -->
Break your aim into three: what you want the group to know, to feel, and to do. Every application question you write later should serve one of these, and Phase 7 checks that all three are covered.

<!-- expandable -->
An aim that stays in the head produces a study that informs but never moves anyone; an aim that is all feeling or all doing floats free of the text. Splitting the aim into know, feel, and do keeps all three honest and tied to the one aim. Know is the truth the passage presses. Feel is the response it seeks, whether comfort, fear, gratitude, or repentance. Do is the concrete change, in thinking, speaking, acting, and loving, and it can be corporate as much as individual. You will not land all three in every question, but across the study they should each get their turn.
