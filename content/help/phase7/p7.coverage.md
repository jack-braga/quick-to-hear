<!-- inline -->
The map shows which verses no question touches. Tag each untouched stretch so you can tell a deliberate gap from one you simply missed.

<!-- expandable -->
Coverage is not about asking a question on every verse; uneven attention is what having an aim looks like. It is about knowing why each gap is there. For every untouched section, choose one: connective tissue that simply carries the argument along, something you are deferring to another week, or a stretch that actually needs a question and was missed. The third tag is the one to watch. It makes the real question concrete: does anything in the passage resist your theme? What resists is often the most important thing to look at again.
