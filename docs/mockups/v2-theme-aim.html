Luke 1:46–55

Theme of the study God scatters the proud and lifts the lowly — covenant mercy that reorders the world.
46Mary said, “My soul magnifies the Lord. 47My spirit has rejoiced in God my Saviour, 48for he has looked at the humble state of his servant… 51He has shown strength with his arm. He has scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts. 52He has put down princes from their thrones, and has exalted the lowly. 53He has filled the hungry with good things. He has sent the rich away empty.
