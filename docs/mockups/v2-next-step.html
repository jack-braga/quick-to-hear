Where should “Next →” live?

You can already jump via the lens rail, but a clear next-step button helps you move through the flow linearly. Here are four placements (each showing the current lens = Survey, next = COMA). Pick one — I can also do a keyboard shortcut alongside it.

A · Floating pill (bottom-right)

Always visible, out of the way. + unobtrusive, familiar. − can overlap content; no “back”.

B · Footer bar (prev · progress · next)

A dedicated step strip. + gives back + forward + a progress sense. − costs a strip of height.

Luke 1:5–25
⚙◉▤▦⊕◎
⁸ Now while he executed the priest’s office before God…
← Read

C · Inline CTA at the end of the step

Sits after the lens’ own content, like “done here?”. + reads as a natural hand-off. − you must scroll to it.

⚙◉▤▦⊕◎

D · Advance arrow in the header rail

A “→” at the end of the lens rail. + tiny, always there, near the steps. − easy to miss; no label.
