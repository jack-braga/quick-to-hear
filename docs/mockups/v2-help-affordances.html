My thoughts / trade-offs

What's good about hiding it all (A–C)
  • Cleanest — nothing competes with the passage or the field (rule 2).
  • Progressive: a glance (hover) vs. a read (click).
  • No Full/Brief mode to explain or maintain.
Two things to weigh
  • Discoverability: the teaching is the tool's substance — hidden behind hover, some users never meet it, especially the make-or-break fields. → why D exists.
  • Touch: phones/tablets have no hover, so tap must open it (A/C collapse to “tap = short, tap again = full”). B already works this way.
My lean: ship B's behaviour (click-to-open, “more” inside) as the engine — it's identical on mouse and touch and needs no hover hacks — with the (i) also opening on hover for mouse users (so it feels like A). Then use D (always-on [I]) for just the two ★★ fields — faithfulness and expected-answer — where users quit. Everything else: (i)-only. Happy to go pure-tooltip (A) if you'd rather keep it uniform.
