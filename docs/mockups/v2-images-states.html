Attach-image — interaction states (slice 2)

The 🖼 add image affordance (Variant A) through its states: an <input type="file" accept="image/*"> behind the button; on pick it downscales + encodes, then the thumbnail lands. Quota comes from navigator.storage.estimate() (reuse useStorageEstimate). Everything below is on a question card; study-notes behave identically.

5 · quota warning (getting full)
What does “power of Elijah” tell us about John?
⚠ Browser storage ~82% full. Big images may not save — this app keeps everything in your browser. Export a copy to be safe.

When estimate() crosses a threshold (~80%): an amber inline note near the add button. Adding is still allowed.

6 · storage full (blocked)
What does “power of Elijah” tell us about John?
◆ Storage is full — can’t add more images. Remove an image, or export your study and clear space.

At the ceiling (or a failed write): the add button disables and a rubric note explains. Never lose existing work — only block new bytes.

7 · rejected file
What does “power of Elijah” tell us about John?
◆That file isn’t an image (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF).
◆Couldn’t read that image — it may be corrupted.

Wrong type or a decode failure: a brief rubric message; nothing is added. (Size is handled by auto-downscale, so “too big” shouldn’t normally appear.)
