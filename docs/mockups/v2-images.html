Images on cards — 2 authoring affordances + the print layout

Images attach to a question or study-note card the same way references do (the ↗ add reference pattern) and print in the handout / leader docs. Left: two ways the attached images could look while authoring. Right: how one renders in the participant handout. Placeholders stand in for the user's own uploaded images.

Variant A: attached images sit as ~90px thumbnails with an inline caption + a delete corner. You see them at a glance; the card grows a little. Good if images are central to the study.

Variant B: attached images show as compact filename chips (like the reference chip), with a tiny preview swatch; the full image only appears in the preview/print. Keeps the card small — best for the “passage stays the subject” rule. Click a chip to preview + edit its caption.
