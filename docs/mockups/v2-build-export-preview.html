Luke 1:5–25 · The forerunner promised

WEBBE · public domain
Theme · aim · to Christ
Theme: God keeps his covenant by sending the forerunner, answering long prayer in his own timing. Aim: trust God's timing where we've prayed long and heard nothing. To Christ: John points past himself to the Lord he prepares.
1. What is Zacharias doing when the angel appears…?
Expected · Ordinary priestly duty at the hour of incense/prayer — the setting says God answers his praying people.

2. …“the spirit and power of Elijah.” What was promised about Elijah?
↗ Support passage — Malachi 4:5–6 (printed for the group)
“…I will send you Elijah… turn the hearts of the fathers to the children…”
↩ Return question set — brings the group home to Luke 1.
Expected · Elijah would come before the Day of the Lord to turn hearts — so John is the promised forerunner.
Luke 1:17 · meaning · ★ essential · 7 min
▣ Commentary — printed for the group
Incense = the hour of prayer; the praying crowd frames the angel's words as answered prayer.
3. …the timing of this answer — what does it show about God?
Expected · God is not slow; he keeps his word and acts at the right time.
